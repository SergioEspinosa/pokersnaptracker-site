Privacy policy

Last updated: October 29, 2026

Poker Snap Tracker does not collect, transmit or sell personal data.

What the app stores

The hands, sessions, bankroll movements, tags and settings you enter. They are stored only on your phone's internal storage.

What it does not do

It requires no account and has no servers. It uses no third-party analytics, ads or trackers, and it does not access your contacts, location, camera or microphone.

Usage timings

The app measures locally how long it takes you to record a hand, to improve the experience. That data does not leave your phone unless you share it.

Export

If you export a backup or a CSV file, you choose where to send it (for example, your storage or a messaging app). From then on, that file is subject to the terms of the destination you choose.

Deletion

Uninstalling the app deletes all of its data from your phone. Without an exported backup, it cannot be recovered.

Children

The app is not intended for anyone under 18.
